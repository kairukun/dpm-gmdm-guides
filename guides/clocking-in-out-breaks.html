Clocking In/Out & Breaks

This guide provides a straightforward process for clocking in and out, as well as managing breaks, ensuring that employees can efficiently track their work hours. It simplifies the steps needed to navigate the home screen and highlights important features, such as job selection and break management. By following this guide, users can avoid confusion and ensure compliance with timekeeping policies, ultimately enhancing productivity and accountability in the workplace.

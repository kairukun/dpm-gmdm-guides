Send Team Portal Reset Emails in RTI/XBO

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to send team portal reset emails in RTI/XBO. It is useful for anyone who needs to reset employee access or multi-factor authentication and wants to learn how to do it efficiently.

Xenial
11 steps · 10 screenshots
  1. 1
    Navigate to https://xbo.xenial.com/login
  2. 2
    Log in.
  3. 3
    Click "Employees && Scheduling"
  4. 4
    Click "Add / Update Employees"
  5. 5
    Search for the employee you need to reset.
  6. 6
    Information: If there is a checkmark, their account is active.
  7. 7
    Verify their email address is correct.

  8. XBO

  9. 8
    Click this icon.
  10. 9
    Click "Reset Access" and/or "Reset MFA" This sends a reset link to their email address.

  11. RTI

  12. 10
    Click the employee you need to reset.
  13. 11
    Click "Reset Access" and/or "Reset MFA" This sends a reset link to their email address.

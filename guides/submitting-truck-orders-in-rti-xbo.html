Submitting Truck Orders in RTI/XBO

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to submit truck orders in RTI/XBO and TRACS. It explains how to navigate the platforms, enter projections, calculate truck orders, and verify and submit the orders. By following this guide, users will be able to efficiently submit their truck orders and ensure accurate inventory management.

Assigning/Unassigning/Naming Equipment in RAM (DMs Only)

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to assign, unassign, and name equipment in RAM, a platform used for managing equipment in Zenput. It explains how to navigate to the RAM platform, use filters to find and assign/unassign equipment, and how to name equipment. This guide is useful for restaurant managers who want to ensure that their equipment is properly assigned and named in Zenput for accurate tracking and management.

Adding RTI Oil Invoice in RTI/XBO

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to add an RTI oil invoice in RTI/XBO. It also includes information on how to find the invoice on RTI TOM and how to count the oil for inventory purposes. Following this guide will help users accurately add and track RTI oil invoices in the system.

XenialRtitom
29 steps · 26 screenshots
  1. 1
    RTI Oil Invoice.
  2. 2
    Navigate to https://xbo.xenial.com/login
  3. 3
    Log in.
  4. 4
    Click "Order/Purchase/Transfer"
  5. 5
    Click "Enter Other Purchases & Credits"
  6. 6
    Click "Add Purchase"
  7. 7
    Select restaurant you are adding the invoice for. Restaurant accounts will only show their restaurant.
  8. 8
    Click "RTI Oil" in "Vendor" field.
  9. 9
    Click "+ Add Item"
  10. 10
    Click "11718673 - Oil - Zero Trans Fat"
  11. 11
    Click "+ Add Item"
  12. 12
    Click "YGCREDIT - Oil-Yellow Grease Credit"
  13. 13
    Put in the oil Qty.
  14. 14
    Put in the credit Qty.
  15. Warning
    Credits should be put in as a negative quantity.
  16. Tip
    Always verify the Cost matches the cost on the invoice.
  17. 15
    Put in the "Balance Amount"
  18. Warning
    The "Balance Amount" should match the invoice amount and match the Total in the bottom right corner.
  19. 16
    Put in the invoice number.
  20. 17
    Put in the date of the invoice.
  21. 18
    Click "Save"

  22. Finding Invoice on RTI TOM

  23. 19
    Navigate to "rtitom.com/LogIn.aspx?ReturnUrl=%2f"
  24. 20
    Log in.
  25. 21
    Click "My Account"
  26. 22
    Click "Billing History"
  27. 23
    Find the restaurant in the "Location" dropdown. Restaurant accounts will only show their restaurant.
  28. 24
    Input the date range for the invoice you are looking for.
  29. 25
    Click "Search"
  30. 26
    Click "View" on the invoice you are searching for.

  31. Counting Oil

  32. 27
    This is display is on the Fresh Oil Tank of every RTI Oil system. This displays the amount of oil in the tank in pounds.
  33. 28
    Add this amount and the amount of oil within the fryer vats, and any additional oil.
  34. 29
    This amount will be entered under "Lbs." for "Oil - Zero Trans Fat" in your inventory counts.

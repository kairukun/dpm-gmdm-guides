Drawers/Deposits in RTI/XBO

This guide provides a step-by-step process for managing cash deposits and safe counts in the XBO system, ensuring accurate financial tracking. By following these instructions, users can efficiently navigate the platform, streamline their cash management tasks, and minimize errors. It's an essential resource for anyone looking to enhance their operational efficiency and maintain financial integrity within their organization.

Making Schedule in RTI/XBO

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to make a schedule in RTI/XBO. It covers everything from logging in to the platform, adding projections, creating manager and crew schedules, and viewing and printing the schedule.

Xenial
50 steps · 48 screenshots Download PDF
  1. Warning
    Always verify your schedule follows all company policies and labor laws before posting.
  2. Warning
    The DM should approve your schedules before posting.
  3. 1
    Navigate to https://xbo.xenial.com/login
  4. 2
    Log in.
  5. 3
    Click "Employees && Scheduling"
  6. 4
    Click "Add/Update Projections"
  7. 5
    Search for restaurant you are making the schedule for. Restaurant accounts will only be able to see their restaurant.
  8. 6
    Click "Add Projections"
  9. 7
    Select a date within the week of the schedule you are making.
  10. 8
    Click "Add"
  11. 9
    Click "Use Trend Values"
  12. Warning
    Please review your projections to make sure they are accurate. See Projection section for information on trend dates.
  13. 10
    Click "Save"

  14. Projections

  15. 11
    Click "Trend Dates"
  16. 12
    Trend Dates preview:
  17. Warning
    The following steps are to show how to change dates being used for trend. This should only be done if you have a busy or slow sales day in your trend.
  18. 13
    Click which date you would like to remove.
  19. 14
    Click down arrow.
  20. 15
    Click date you want to add to your trend dates.
  21. 16
    Click up arrow.
  22. 17
    Click "Save"

  23. Manager Schedule

  24. 18
    Click "Manager Schedules"
  25. 19
    Select restaurant for the schedule you are making. Restaurant accounts will be able to see their restaurant.
  26. 20
    Select the 4 week period you would like to make the manager schedule for.
  27. 21
    Input the schedule for the managers.
  28. Tip
    You will type the scheduled hours in. Examples: 11-5p will show 11:00AM - 05:00PM 5p-1 will show 05:00PM - 01:00AM
  29. 22
    Click "Save"

  30. Crew Schedule

  31. 23
    Click "Crew Schedules"
  32. 24
    Click "Add Schedule"
  33. 25
    Select restaurant for the schedule you are making. Restaurant accounts will be able to see their restaurant.
  34. 26
    Select a date within the week of the schedule you are making.
  35. 27
    Click "Add"

  36. Daily View

  37. 28
    Preview of the schedule builder in Daily View (default view).
  38. 29
    Breakdown of the schedule builder in Daily View (default view).
  39. Warning
    If your sales projections stop earlier than the close time of your restaurant, RTI has the incorrect Hours of Operations. A DO will have to fix this for you.
  40. 30
    Schedules on daily view (which is the default appearance) are line bars. You will left click and drag from start to finish to create the schedule shifts.
  41. 31
    You can also type the shift on the left side in the shift column.
  42. 32
    Right clicking on a shift gives options for that shift.
  43. 33
    Click "Load Prior" if you want to copy a schedule from a previous day or week.
  44. 34
    Click either "Day" or "Week"
  45. 35
    Select the date(s) you want to copy.
  46. 36
    Click "Apply"
  47. 37
    Click "Save" once you have completed the schedule for the current day.

  48. Weekly View

  49. 38
    You can also create or view a schedule by Weekly View. On any day, click "Weekly View"
  50. 39
    Preview of the schedule builder in Weekly View.
  51. Tip
    The header is the same between Daily and Weekly View.
  52. 40
    On Weekly View, all shifts are adding by typing.
  53. Tip
    You will type the scheduled hours in. Examples: 11-5p will show 11:00AM - 05:00PM 5p-1 will show 05:00PM - 01:00AM
  54. 41
    Click "Employees"
  55. 42
    Click here.
  56. 43
    Click "Weekly View"
  57. 44
    Click "Save" once you have completed the schedule for the week.

  58. Complete/Approve

  59. Warning
    This must be done for the schedule to generate on Team Portal.
  60. 45
    Navigate to https://xbo.xenial.com/labor/schedules/details
  61. 46
    Once the schedule is completed, Click "Completed"
  62. 47
    DM Only Once the schedule has been reviewed, Click "Approved"

  63. Print Schedule

  64. 48
    Click "This Week's Schedule" or "Next Week's Schedule"
  65. 49
    Click Print.
  66. 50
    Click "Print"

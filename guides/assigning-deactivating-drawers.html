Assigning/Deactivating Drawers

This guide provides essential instructions for managing drawers in a point-of-sale system, including assigning, reassigning, unassigning, and deactivating drawers. It ensures that only clocked-in employees can be assigned to a drawer, maintaining security and accountability. By following this guide, users can efficiently manage cash drawers, ensuring proper cash handling and compliance with policies. It's a valuable resource for anyone looking to streamline their cash management processes.

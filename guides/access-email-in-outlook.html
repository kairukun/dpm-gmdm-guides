Access Email in Outlook

This guide provides a comprehensive overview of how to access your email using both Outlook.com and the Outlook desktop and mobile apps. It streamlines the login process with step-by-step instructions tailored for different devices and situations, making it easy for users of all skill levels to connect to their accounts securely. Whether you're a new user or just need a refresher, this guide ensures a smooth email access experience. View it to enhance your productivity and manage your communications effectively.

  1. Accessing Outlook.com

  2. 1
    Navigate to https://outlook.office.com
  3. 2
    Click the "Email, phone, or Skype" field.
  4. 3
    Click "Next"
  5. Tip
    If it asks, select the option for School/Work account, then click Next.
  6. 4
    Click "Sign In"
  7. 5
    Click "Yes" if you are on a private computer.\ Click "No" if you are on a public/office computer.

  9. 6
    Click Windows icon
  10. 7
    Click "Outlook" You may have to search for it or download it.
  11. 8
    Put in your email address.
  12. 9
    Click "Continue"
  13. 10
    Put in your password. Click "Sign in"
  14. 11
    Click "Yes" if you are on a private computer.\ Click "No" if you are on a public/office computer.

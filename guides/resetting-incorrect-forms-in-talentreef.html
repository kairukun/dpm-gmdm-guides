Resetting Incorrect Forms in TalentReef

This guide provides a step-by-step process for resetting incorrect forms in TalentReef, ensuring that employees can easily correct any mistakes in their onboarding paperwork. By following these instructions, users can efficiently navigate the system to reset forms like the I-9 and W-4, facilitating a smoother onboarding experience. It is essential for HR personnel and managers to understand this process to maintain compliance and accuracy in employee documentation.

TalentreefJobappnetwork
18 steps · 15 screenshots

  1. Find Employee

  2. 1
    Navigate to https://login.jobappnetwork.com/login
  3. 2
    Click "Log in"
  4. 3
    Click "Jump to"
  5. 4
    Click "Hire"
  6. 5
    Click "Onboarding"
  7. 6
    Click on the employee that needs a form reset.
  8. Tip
    You may also use the search bar for locate the employee.

  9. Resetting Forms (besides I9)

  10. 7
    Complete "Find Employee" section.
  11. 8
    Click "Forms"
  12. 9
    Click this link next to the form that needs reset.
  13. 10
    Click "Ok"
  14. Warning
    Once reset, they will have to re-login to their account to refill out the forms.
  15. Warning
    If it is a form that we have to complete (I9, W4, etc), you will have to recomplete your section.

  16. Resetting I9

  17. 11
    Complete "Find Employee" section.
  18. 12
    Click "I9"
  19. 13
    If employee made a mistake on their section of the I9, click the "If the new hire committed a mistake on Section 1 of the I-9, including Supplement A (if applicable)" option.
  20. 14
    Click "Reset I9"
  21. Warning
    Once reset, they will have to re-login to their account to refill out the forms.
  22. Warning
    If it is a form that we have to complete (I9, W4, etc), you will have to recomplete your section.
  23. 15
    If we made a mistake on our section of the I9, click the "If you committed a mistake on Section 2 of the I-9" option.
  24. 16
    Click "Next"
  25. 17
    Edit the information that needs corrected.
  26. 18
    Click "Finish" once corrected.

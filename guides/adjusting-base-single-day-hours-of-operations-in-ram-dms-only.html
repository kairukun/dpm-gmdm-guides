Adjusting Base/Single Day Hours of Operations in RAM (DMs only)

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to adjust the base and single day hours of operations in RAM. By following these steps, users can easily navigate through the necessary menus and make changes to the opening and closing times for specific days, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information for their restaurant.

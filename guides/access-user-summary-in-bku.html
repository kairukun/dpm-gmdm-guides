Access User Summary in BKU

This guide provides a straightforward method for accessing and reviewing user summaries in the Burger King University platform. It outlines essential steps to track user engagement, including login history, video progress, course statuses, and earned badges. By following this guide, you can efficiently monitor user activity and achievements, ensuring effective training management and support for your team.

Adjust KDS Routing

This guide provides a straightforward process for adjusting KDS routing, essential for optimizing kitchen operations. By following the steps outlined, users can efficiently manage their KDS settings, ensuring that orders are routed correctly and enhancing overall workflow. Whether you're a manager or staff member, mastering this function can lead to improved service and reduced errors in order preparation. Access the guide for a clear, step-by-step approach to streamline your kitchen's performance.

Creating a New Email Rule in Outlook

This guide provides a straightforward method for creating custom email rules in Outlook, enhancing your email management efficiency. By following these steps, you can tailor your inbox to filter and organize messages according to your specific needs, saving you time and reducing clutter. Whether you're looking to prioritize important emails or automate repetitive tasks, this guide equips you with the tools to optimize your Outlook experience.

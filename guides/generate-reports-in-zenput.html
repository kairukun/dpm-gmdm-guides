Generate Reports in Zenput

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to access and generate reports in Zenput. It covers both custom reports and form reports, explaining how to export data and filter results. By following this guide, users will be able to efficiently generate and analyze reports in Zenput to track and manage their data effectively.

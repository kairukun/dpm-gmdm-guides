Rerouting Printers

This guide provides a straightforward process for rerouting printers, ensuring efficient management of printing tasks in a point-of-sale system. By following these simple steps, users can quickly redirect print jobs to the desired printer, minimizing downtime and enhancing productivity. It's an essential resource for anyone looking to streamline their printing operations in a busy environment.

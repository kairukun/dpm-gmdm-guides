4000 Frymaster Fryers

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for adding or editing products in 4000 Frymaster Fryers. It covers various settings such as temperature, hold time, shake timers, and filter settings. It also includes instructions for quick filtration, cleaning and filtering, filling the vat from bulk, and pan to waste processes. If you need to learn how to manage and operate a Frymaster Fryer, this guide is a valuable resource.

  1. Adding or Editing Existing Products

  2. 1

  15. Warning

Press the X (NO) button to run the pump again if the oil level is below the top oil level full line. \* Press the √ (check - YES) button once the oil level is at the top oil level full line. If the vat oil level is not completely filled, check the filter pan to see if most of the oil has returned. The pan may have a small amount of oil. Press the √ (check - YES) button once no oil remains in the filter pan.

  101. Fill Vat from Bulk

  102. 85
    Turn the fryer off.
  103. 86
    Press the filration menu.
  104. 87
    Scroll down.
  105. 88
    Select "FILL VAT FROM BULK"
  106. 89
    FILL VAT FROM BULK? Press the √ (check - YES) button to continue. If the X (NO) button is selected, the controller goes to OFF.
  107. 90
    START FILLING?\ PRESS AND HOLD Press and hold the button to fill the vat. The bulk oil refill pump uses a momentary switch. It only pumps if the switch is depressed.
  108. 91
    RELEASE BUTTON WHEN FULL Release the button when the vat is filled to the lower fill line.
  109. 92
    CONTINUE FILLING? Press the X (NO) button once the vat is full to exit and return to OFF. Press the √ (check - YES) button to continue filling.
  110. 93
    Turn the fryer back on.

  112. 94

  113. 95

  114. 96

  115. 97
    PAN TO WASTE? Press the √ (check - YES) button to continue. If the X (NO) button is selected, the user returns to the filtration menu. If no pan is detected, the controller displays INSERT PAN until the pan is detected.
  116. 98
    BULK TANK FULL? Press the √ (check - YES) button to acknowledge and call the bulk oil waste provider. The display returns to OFF.
  117. 99
    OPEN DISPOSE VALVE Open the left cabinet door and unlock the valve if necessary. Pull the dispose valve completely forward to start disposal.
  118. 100
    DISPOSING No action is required while the pump transfers the waste oil from the pan to the bulk oil waste tanks for four (4) minutes.
  119. 101
    REMOVE PAN Carefully pull the filter pan from the fryer.
  120. Warning
    Open the filter pan slowly to avoid splashing of hot oil that may cause severe burns, slipping and falling.
  121. 102
    IS PAN EMPTY? If the filter pan is empty press the √ (check - YES) button. If the pan is not empty, press the X (NO) button and return to the previous step.
  122. 103
    INSET PAN Insert the pan.
  123. 104
    CLOSE DISPOSE VALVE Close the dispose valve by pushing the valve handle toward the rear of the fryer until it stops. Relock the valve if required by your manager.
  124. 105
    Turn the fryer on.

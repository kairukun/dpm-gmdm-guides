Manager/Employee Discounts and Guest Recovery

This guide provides a straightforward process for applying manager and employee discounts, as well as handling guest recovery efficiently. By following these steps, staff can ensure accurate transactions while maximizing customer satisfaction. It simplifies the discounting process, making it easier for employees to manage orders and enhance the guest experience. Viewing this guide will help streamline operations and improve service quality in your establishment.

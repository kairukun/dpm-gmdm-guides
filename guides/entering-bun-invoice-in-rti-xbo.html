Entering Bun Invoice in RTI/XBO

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to enter a bun invoice in RTI/XBO. It ensures accuracy by verifying the quantity and cost per tray on the invoice. Following these steps will help maintain accurate records and prevent errors in the accounting system.

Xenial
14 steps · 13 screenshots
  1. Warning
    Always verify the number of buns brought by the bakery matches the invoice quantity.
  2. 1
    Example Bakery Invoice from Flowers.
  3. 2
    Navigate to https://xbo.xenial.com/login
  4. 3
    Click "Log In"
  5. 4
    Click "Order/Purchase/Transfer"
  6. 5
    Click "Enter Other Purchases & Credits"
  7. 6
    Click "Add Purchase"
  8. 7
    Select restaurant you are adding the invoice for. Restaurant accounts only show their restaurant.
  9. 8
    Select the bakery you received the bread from.
  10. 9
    Enter the invoice number.
  11. 10
    Click "Load Default Items"
  12. 11
    Verify the "Cost" fields match the price per tray on the invoice.
  13. 12
    Enter the quantity of buns you received from the bun invoice.
  14. 13
    Enter the balance amount from the invoice.
  15. Warning
    The "Total" field should match the invoice. If it does not match, the "Cost" or "Quantity" fields are not correct. Occassionally, the amount may be off by 1 cent, that is unavoidable.
  16. 14
    Click "Save"

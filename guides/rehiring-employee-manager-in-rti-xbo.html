Rehiring Employee/Manager in RTI/XBO

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to rehire an employee in RTI/XBO. It includes detailed steps on navigating the platform, updating employee status, and verifying information. Viewing this guide will help you easily and accurately rehire employees, ensuring all necessary information is correct.

Xenial
13 steps · 12 screenshots
  1. Warning
    RTI may have a slightly different setup on menus, but the same information must be filled out on either system.
  2. 1
    Navigate to https://xbo.xenial.com/login
  3. 2
    Login.
  4. 3
    Click "Employees && Scheduling"
  5. 4
    Click "Add / Update Employees"
  6. 5
    Click "Inactive"
  7. 6
    Click "Terminated"
  8. 7
    Search for the employee you are rehiring.
  9. 8
    Click "Search"
  10. 9
    Click the employee you are rehiring.
  11. 10
    Click "Status"
  12. 11
    Click the "Active" switch to turn it to "Yes"
  13. 12
    Verify all the information is correct under all sections.
  14. Warning
    Pay Rate and Payroll ID must be 100% accurate.
  15. 13
    Click "Save"

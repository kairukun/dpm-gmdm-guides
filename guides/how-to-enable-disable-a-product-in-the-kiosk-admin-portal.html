Managing Products in the Kiosk Admin Portal

This guide provides essential instructions for effectively managing products in the Kiosk Admin Portal, ensuring users can easily mark items as sold out or hidden. It simplifies the process of navigating the portal, searching for products, and updating their status, which is crucial for maintaining accurate inventory. By following these steps, users can enhance their kiosk's functionality and improve customer experience. Overall, this guide is a valuable resource for anyone looking to streamline product management in the kiosk environment.

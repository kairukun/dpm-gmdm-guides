Managing Count Sheets in RTI/XBO

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to manage count sheets in RTI/XBO, a restaurant management system. It covers how to add/remove items from weekly and daily counts, and sort forms. If you need to effectively manage count sheets in RTI/XBO, this guide is a valuable resource.

  9. Add/Remove- Daily Counts

  10. 8
    Click "Additional Daily Items"
  11. 9
    Search the item you are adding or removing.
  12. 10
    Click the switch for the item you want counted. Uncheck the box to remove the item.
  13. 11
    Click "Save"

  14. Sorting Forms

  15. 12
    Click "List Order"
  16. 13
    Click the "Locations" field to switch between sections of the sheet.
  17. 14
    Drag the items in the order you want them displayed.
  18. 15
    Click "Save"

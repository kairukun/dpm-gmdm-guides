Proctor ServSafe Test in ServSafe (Proctors Only) (INCOMPLETE)

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to proctor the ServSafe test. It outlines the process of setting up the exam for the proctor and examinees, including logging in, scheduling the exam session, entering the proctor code, and accessing the exam. It is a useful resource for anyone who needs to administer the ServSafe test and ensure a smooth testing experience.

    Navigate to https://www.servsafe.com/

    Click "LOGIN / CREATE ACCOUNT"

    Log in to the proctor account.

  7. 6

  8. 7

  9. 8

  10. 9

  11. 10

    Enter how many people are being proctored.
  14. 13
    Select the date of the exam.
  15. 14
    Click "SCHEDULE EXAM SESSION"
  16. 15
    Your proctor code will be on this page. It will also be sent in email.

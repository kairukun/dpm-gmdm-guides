Generating SOS Reports in DMB Portal

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to generate a Drive Thru Daypart Summary Report. By following these steps, users can easily navigate to the desired website, select the appropriate options, and view the report for a specific date range. The report includes important metrics such as MBT, PUT, and TTL, providing valuable insights into drive-thru performance. This guide is essential for anyone looking to analyze and optimize their drive-thru operations.

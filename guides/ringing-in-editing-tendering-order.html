Ringing in/Editing/Tendering Order

This guide provides a comprehensive overview of the order ringing, editing, and tendering process, making it essential for anyone working in a fast-paced food service environment. It details each step clearly, from starting an order to completing transactions, ensuring that employees can efficiently manage customer orders and payments. By following this guide, staff can enhance their operational skills, improve customer service, and streamline the ordering process, ultimately leading to a better dining experience.

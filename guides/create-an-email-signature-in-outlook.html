Create an Email Signature in Outlook

This guide provides a straightforward approach to creating a professional email signature in Outlook, ensuring your communications reflect your brand effectively. It covers both desktop and app versions, making it accessible for all users. By following these steps, you can personalize your signature with specific colors and set defaults for new messages and replies, enhancing your email's visual consistency and professionalism.

  1. Desktop App

  2. 1
    Navigate to https://outlook.office.com/mail/
  3. 2
    Click here.
  4. 3
    Click "Account"
  5. 4
    Click "Signatures"
  6. 5
    Name signature.
  7. 6
    Put in signature details.
  8. Tip
    Make sure that your name and Ampler is colored Ampler Green. Hex #73A74D. R115, G167, B77.
  9. 7
    Select default signature "For New Messages"
  10. 8
    Select default signature "For Replies/Forwards"
  11. 9
    Click "Save"

  12. Outlook App

  13. 10
    Click Window icon
  14. 11
    Click Settings icon.
  15. 12
    Click "Signatures"
  16. 13
    Input your section.
  17. Tip
    Depending on your device, you may have to copy your signature from a sent email and paste from clipboard to have it match properly.
